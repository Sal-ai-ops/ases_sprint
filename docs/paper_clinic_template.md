---

### 1. Threat Model
*What can the adversary do, what do they know, and what is their explicit goal? State it in one concise paragraph.*

### 2. Central Falsifiable Claim
*What is the single, testable sentence the paper is trying to establish?*

### 3. Core Method
*How does the mechanism work in 5 technical sentences that a colleague could reimplement?*

### 4. Evaluation Rigor
*What was measured, on what datasets, against which baselines, and with what statistical confidence intervals?*

### 5. Strongest Contribution
*What concrete defensive capability does this paper make possible that was not possible before? (Must be completed BEFORE Question 6).*

### 6. Weakest Point / Failure Mode
*Where does the evaluation fail? (e.g., temperature=0 testing, missing adaptive attacks, unrealistic threat model, unmeasured over-refusal).*

### 7. Reproducibility Audit
*Is there a public container/codebase? Are dependencies pinned by digest? Can Table 1 be reproduced in under 4 hours?*

### 8. Engineering Transfer to nGOT
*What specific architectural change or pipeline control should our team adopt based on this paper?*
