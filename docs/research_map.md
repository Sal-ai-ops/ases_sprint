# Research Landscape Map — Week 1 Integration

## 1. Institutional Mapping

### CISPA Helmholtz Center (Saarbrücken)
- **Trustworthy & Private ML:** Focuses on membership inference, memorization extraction, and limits of differential privacy.
- **Automated Debugging & Program Analysis:** Delta debugging (minimizing adversarial inputs) and grammar-based fuzzing.
- **Reliable Security Guarantees:** Formal methods applied to non-generative system scaffolding.

### SnT — University of Luxembourg
- **TruX (Trustworthy Software):** Automated vulnerability repair, code representation learning, and benchmark contamination auditing.
- **CritiX (Critical & Extreme Security):** Intrusion tolerance — assuming planner compromise and bounding blast radius.
- **SVV (Software Verification & Validation):** Traceable requirements engineering for regulated/medical software.
